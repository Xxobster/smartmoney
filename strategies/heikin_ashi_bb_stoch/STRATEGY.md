## Stated rules

- Cryptocurrency; examples on 1-hour Bitcoin
- Trend: Exponential Moving Average (EMA) 40 vs Simple Moving Average (SMA) 50
- Touch Bollinger Band + Stochastic Relative Strength Index 20/80 + reclaim + Heikin Ashi color flip
- Stop at extreme of the touch candle
- Take-profit 1:1 or opposite Heikin Ashi color

## Fair H0 interpretation

- Bollinger length **20**, 2 standard deviations (not stated; frozen default)
- Stochastic Relative Strength Index: RSI 13, stoch 10, K/D 3
- Touch may occur on the signal bar or up to 5 bars earlier
- Full position take-profit at 1R (Heikin Ashi color trail omitted)
- Long and short reported **separately** — never pooled
