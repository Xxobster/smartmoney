## H0 (long-biased; shorts mirrored)

Executable score proxies (video’s 7 tests compressed):

1. Trend: close > EMA(50)  
2. Build-up: 8-bar range width < 0.7 × ATR(14) before the break  
3. Break: close beyond prior 20-bar high/low with close in outer 30% of bar  
4. Volume: volume > 1.5 × SMA(20) volume on break bar  
5. Retest: within 8 bars, touch the broken level and close back through it  
6. Follow-through: next bar closes in break direction  

Enter on the follow-through bar if all six are true. Stop beyond retest extreme; Take-Profit Reward:Risk **2.0**.

Timeframes: **15m**, **1h**. Symbols: `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`.
