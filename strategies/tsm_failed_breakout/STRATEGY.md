## H0

- Sweep: close beyond prior 20-bar high/low  
- Fail: within **3** bars, close back inside the range  
- Short after failed upside break; long after failed downside break  
- Optional filter: volume on sweep bar > SMA(20) volume  
- Stop beyond sweep extreme; Take-Profit Reward:Risk **2.0**  
- Timeframes: **15m**, **1h**  

Symbols: `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`
