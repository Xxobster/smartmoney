## H0

- Classic monthly pivot from **prior** completed calendar month Open-High-Low-Close  
- Bollinger Bands: Simple Moving Average 50, 2σ  
- Long: close above monthly pivot; bar touches lower or middle band; bullish close  
- Short: close below pivot; bar touches upper or middle band; bearish close  
- Stop beyond signal-bar extreme; Take-Profit Reward:Risk **2.0**  
- Timeframe: **1d** (also probe **4h** with same monthly pivot series)

Symbols: `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`
